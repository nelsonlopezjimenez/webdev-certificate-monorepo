For a **teaching context**, ESLint still wins — but it's close.

| | ESLint | Biome |
|--|--------|-------|
| **Speed** | Slow on large projects | 10–100x faster |
| **Config** | Verbose (`eslint.config.js`) | Simple, one `biome.json` |
| **Prettier replacement** | No (needs Prettier too) | Yes (lints + formats) |
| **Plugin ecosystem** | Massive | Small but growing |
| **VS Code extension** | Excellent, mature | Good, improving |
| **Industry adoption** | Dominant | Growing fast |
| **Beginner-friendly** | ❌ Config is intimidating | ✅ Works out of the box |

**Biome's pitch:** one tool replaces both ESLint + Prettier, near-zero config, blazing fast.

**ESLint's counter:** students will encounter it everywhere — jobs, open source, tutorials. It's the industry standard today.

**My take for CIS 243:** teach ESLint because your students will see it in every job listing and every existing project. But mention Biome as "what's coming" — it's worth a 10-minute demo in Week 1 alongside the extensions setup. In 2–3 years the answer may flip.

If you want to try Biome today in your own workflow, it's genuinely excellent.
